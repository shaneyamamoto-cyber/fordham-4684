# Photoreal pass — image-to-image brief

**The planner is the source of truth.** Every dimension, position and finish comes from
`Elevation Planner.dc.html`. Nothing is restated here — for the measured spec, copy the
text from the planner's PROMPT tab and paste it under the prompt below.

Use `render-plate.png` as the **structure/depth reference**. The geometry in it is
measured and must not move. Repaint materials and lighting only.

## Prompt

> Photorealistic interior photograph of a small attic bathroom, shot on a 35mm lens at
> eye height from the doorway. Keep the exact camera angle, room proportions and object
> placement of the reference image — do not move, resize, add or remove anything.
>
> Materials and finishes exactly as specified in the accompanying schedule. Read every
> dimension from the reference image, not from convention.
>
> Lighting: soft daylight from behind the camera, warm LED accents where the design has
> them, no invented fixtures. Natural contact shadows.
>
> Calm, editorial, restrained — architectural magazine photography, not a CGI showroom.

## Negative

wide-angle distortion, fisheye, extra fixtures, extra shelves, changed cabinet layout,
plants, towels, bottles, people, text, watermark, glossy CGI plastic, HDR halos.

## Re-shooting the plate

The 3D reads the **locked version**, so re-lock in the planner after any change, reload
`Bathroom 3D.html`, orbit to the view you want, and grab a fresh plate.
