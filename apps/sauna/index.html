YamaZina Sauna — Mark 5 — Closed Box BenchThe Elements list is right here — click any numbered piece below, or any object in the 3D view, to open its move/rotate/resize/copy/delete toolbar.
Shed frame as built: 10'4" x 6'4", 9'6" front (LW1, door wall) / 7'10" back (LW2, tank-window wall).
Finished interior (after 3" floor build-up + insulated cedar T&G walls/ceiling): 10'1" x 6'1" (~61.3 sq ft), 9'2" front / 7'5" back, ~509 cu ft — use this volume for heater sizing.
Wall naming (Rev. B): LW1 (Long Wall 1) = the door's wall, now the designated front. LW2 (Long Wall 2) = the opposite wall with the tank-window clerestory, now the designated back. SW1 (Short Wall 1) = the bench-return wall, now the left wall (was called "right" before this revision). SW2 (Short Wall 2) = the wall next to the door, now the right wall (was called "left" before this revision). The default 3D view now looks in from LW1/the door side, matching this.
Heater
YamaZina Mark 2 spec, Rev. B floor plan — on LW1 (the front wall, the same wall as the door), clear of both the door/landing platform and SW1's bench footprint, 6"-12" standoff off the wall. HUUM Hive Mini (9kW) or HUUM Hive (11kW) — same ~18.1" drum, 30" top-of-rock reference height. Wood guard rebuilt tighter to the barrel (2" clearance, was 4") and taller (22", was 16"), now with horizontal slats instead of vertical. Pick the exact model in the Elements tab.
Door & entry
YamaZina Mark 2 spec, Rev. B floor plan — LW1 (front wall), 6" off SW2 (the right wall): fixed 24" wide x 72" tall cedar door, lightly frosted glass window (top half by default, at least 5" of wood on every side of the glass). Now floats 10" above the sauna floor instead of sitting at deck height — a cedar landing platform on the floor underneath bridges the gap.
Bench
YamaZina Mark 2 spec, Rev. B floor plan — the L-shaped bench moved off LW1 onto LW2 (the back, 10'4" tank-window wall) and SW1 (the left, 6'4" wall), running the full length of both with no door-clearance cutout needed on either run anymore: "foot bench" (lower tier, 32" seat) plus "top bench" (upper tier, 50" seat — 18" above the foot bench, fixed 24" deep). The foot bench reaches 44" off the wall (20" exposed past the top bench's edge, tucking 6" under it). Framed like a floor (12" max joist spacing) with 4"-wide 3/4"-thick deck boards on top and 6"-wide 1/2"-thick front fascia cladding, for 8" total thickness from the front view. The SW2 (right-wall) entrance bench is deleted. Closed box plinth with horizontal slat-clad fronts (small gaps between courses), an open riser gap between the low and high tiers so the high tier doesn't overhang the low seat.
Landing platform
New in Rev. B — front-right corner (LW1/SW2), on the floor under the floating door: 3' wide (along LW1) x 4' deep (along SW2) x 4-1/4" tall, cedar 2x4-on-edge frame with 3/4" cedar deck boards. Width/depth are adjustable within a small range from its own Elements panel; height is fixed to the spec.
Ceiling
New in Rev. B — flattened to 7'8" across the whole build, with a short ~10" transition at LW2 (the back, 10'4" wall) where the natural roof slope takes back over.
Window
SW1 (left wall), above the bench — wide, single unbroken pane (no center mullion). Click it in the 3D view, or pick it from the Elements tab, for Clear / Frosted / Ocean Tint / Glass Block options. The SW2 "tank window" glass-block clerestory has been removed (it looked through to a bathroom that's no longer part of the plan).
